**Sunset Coast** is a 2D top-down open-world crime game. One invented city on a beach at golden hour, five story jobs from the fixer Lena Solis, a five-star wanted system with police chases, busted and wasted, traffic, pedestrians, taxis, hidden parcels, a paint shop, a car dealer and a safehouse — and a day/night clock that slides from golden hour through sunset into a lamplit night. Everything is hand-drawn on canvas in vanilla JavaScript, with procedural WebAudio sound and no network requests at all: the whole game plays offline from `game/index.html`.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

## How it plays

- **WASD / arrows** — drive or walk. **E** — get in or out of a car, talk, buy. **SPACE** — handbrake in a car, punch on foot. **SHIFT** — run. **P** — pause. **M** — mute.
- Steal the red Comet to start the first job, then work through the Impound Heist, the Getaway, the Harbor Run and King of the Coast.
- Earn cash from jobs, taxi fares and parcels; spend it on repairs, repaints and new cars at the dealer. Getting busted sends your car to the impound lot; getting wasted sends you to the hospital. Both cost money.
- Progress saves automatically in the browser.
